# Development screenshots and videos

Store chronological visual evidence of development, UI, debugging and test runs. Name assets YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSSZ_phase-or-issue_short-description.ext (UTC); use PNG/WebP for images and MP4/WebM for video. Link from corresponding issues or audit reports with commit SHA, operating mode and observed behavior. Never upload credentials, personal data or unrelated files. Concept art must be labeled as such. Prefer compressed short recordings and keep large generated artifacts outside Git.
